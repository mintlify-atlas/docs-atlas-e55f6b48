## Purpose

LInjector exists as a transparent, auditable alternative to closed-source executor tools. Its goal is to give developers who work in the Roblox scripting ecosystem a customizable IDE they can inspect, modify, and build themselves. Because the full source is available, you can verify exactly what the software does before running it — there are no hidden behaviors, no bundled binaries, and no obfuscated code.

## No malware guarantee
